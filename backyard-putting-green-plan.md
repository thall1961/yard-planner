# Backyard Putting Green Plan (Simplest Path)

**Location:** Burleson, TX
**Recommendation:** Build a **synthetic (artificial turf) putting green.**

A real grass green in North Texas means TifDwarf/TifEagle bermuda mowed at ⅛" with a $500+ reel mower, daily summer mowing, fungicides, and dedicated irrigation — a part-time job. A synthetic green is roughly a one-weekend build, needs zero mowing, and putts true year-round.

## Build steps

1. **Layout.** Mark the shape with a garden hose — kidney/organic shapes look better than rectangles and hide seams. Practical size: **10'×15' to 12'×20'**. Use the flattest part of the section.
2. **Kill and strip.** Spray the footprint with glyphosate (bermuda dies fast in summer heat — one advantage of building now). Wait a week, then strip the top 3–4" of sod/soil. A sod cutter rental makes this fast.
3. **Edging.** Install bender board or steel edging around the perimeter to contain the base.
4. **Base — the step that determines putt quality.** Lay **3–4" of crushed limestone fines / decomposed granite** (readily available in DFW), moisten, and compact with a rented plate compactor. Screed smooth with a 2×4 and a level. Build in a slight crown or ~1% slope for drainage; sculpt one gentle break if you want. On expansive clay, a thick, properly compacted base is what keeps the green from heaving — **don't skimp here**.
5. **Turf.** Buy **dedicated putting green turf** (nylon or polypropylene putting pile, ~½" — not regular landscape turf). SGC, Purchase Green, and several DFW-local suppliers sell it by the roll. Stretch, trim to the edging, and secure with 5–6" non-galvanized nails every ~6" around the perimeter.
6. **Cups.** Before final securing, place 2–3 regulation 4" cups: cut the turf over each spot and set the cup sleeves into the compacted base flush with the surface.
7. **Infill and roll.** Broadcast a thin layer of dry silica sand, broom it in, and roll it. Sand + rolling sets the green speed — more of both = faster.
8. **Optional:** a strip of taller fringe turf around the edge so you can chip onto the green.

## Cost

| Approach | Cost (~200 sq ft) |
|---|---|
| DIY | **$1,200–2,500** (turf is the big line item at $3–6/sq ft; base material, edging, cups, and tool rentals make up the rest) |
| Professional install (DFW) | $4,000–7,000 |

## Rentals / tools needed

- Sod cutter (strip-out day)
- Plate compactor (base day)
- 2×4 + level for screeding, push broom, utility knife, hammer

## If you really want real grass instead

The only sane version: let the existing bermuda fill the area, then maintain it at ~½" with a reel mower as a "chipping/putting-ish" surface. Fun, but nowhere near true-rolling. For actual putting in this climate, synthetic is the right call.
